### Decision 2: Fault-Stick Coordinate Mapping (RESOLVED)

**Ambiguity resolved:** `.dat` z column is **sample index**, not milliseconds.

**Evidence:**
- z values 202–307 × 4 ms/sample = 808–1228 ms (matches `Volve_Fault_Sticks_synthetic.txt` UTM Z_ms 700–852 ms range)
- All other `.dat` columns confirmed 0-based volume indices

**Canonical mapping (implemented in `streamlit_app.py`):**
```
abs_inline = 1001 + dat_inline_col
abs_crossline = 1900 + dat_crossline_col
twt_ms = dat_z_col * 4.0
```
